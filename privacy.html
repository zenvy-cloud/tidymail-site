Tidymail Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Tidymail helps you find and clear junk email in your Gmail account. It runs entirely on your phone.

What the app accesses

With your permission, the app uses the Gmail API to read email metadata only:

  • From your inbox: sender, subject, date, size, Gmail labels, unsubscribe headers, and the technical headers used to spot phishing (Authentication-Results, Reply-To, In-Reply-To, References, Received).
  • From sent mail: the names and addresses in To and Cc.

It never reads message bodies or attachments. Google's metadata permission doesn't allow it.

If you choose to clean up, the app asks for a second permission so it can move the emails you confirm to Trash, Spam or a Gmail folder. It cannot permanently delete email.

How the data is used

Only to show you which senders look like junk or phishing and to carry out the cleanup you approve. It isn't used for advertising, isn't sold, and isn't used to train any AI model.

Where the data is stored

  • Only on your phone, encrypted with AES-256. The key is kept in Android Keystore or iOS Keychain and is excluded from backups.
  • There is no server, analytics or advertising SDK. The app talks only to Google, and, if you tap one-click Unsubscribe, sends one request to that sender's own unsubscribe address.

Sharing

None. No data is shared with anyone.

Deleting your data

Choose Delete saved scan or Disconnect & revoke all access in the app's Privacy screen, or uninstall the app. You can also remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Tidymail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
